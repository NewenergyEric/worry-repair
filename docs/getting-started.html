What counts as success?怎样算走通了？

The same-browser note and saved card survive a reload, both languages are readable, the film plays, and the GitHub release subscription opens. No contact form or email signup runs in this demo.同一浏览器刷新后仍能看到笔记与收藏；两种语言可读，视频可播放，GitHub 版本订阅可打开。示例不运行联系表单或邮件订阅。
