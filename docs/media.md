The English and Chinese READMEs show a **12-second looping GIF** (800×450, 10 fps, no audio) featuring the shop, three cards and an if–then example. The previews are 842 KB in English and 802 KB in Chinese, compared with about 6.4 MB for each complete film. Each README links to its full 68-second film without loading an inline MP4 player.

Two 1280×720 still images accompany each language: the three cards at **00:20** in the film, and the live website's **Life was never single choice / 人生，又不是单选题** card. Each JPEG is about 98–107 KB. These are finished previews and screenshots; the editable production files remain private.

GIF playback follows the visitor's [GitHub motion preference](https://docs.github.com/en/account-and-profile/how-tos/account-settings/managing-accessibility-settings#managing-motion). No click is needed when animated images are enabled. The static screenshots remain readable when animation is disabled.

The demo still includes its local landscape player. The original MP4 exports and other aspect ratios are retained for full viewing and social publishing. These README changes do not update the official website.
